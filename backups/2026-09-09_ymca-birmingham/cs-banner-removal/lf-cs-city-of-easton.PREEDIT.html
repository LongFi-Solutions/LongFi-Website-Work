The Challenge

The downtown cell network cannot keep up with festival crowds.

When the streets fill, residents and visitors cannot get texts out, families lose track of each other in the crowd, and calls will not connect.

Easton sits at the confluence of the Delaware and Lehigh rivers, a historic downtown that packs tens of thousands into a few walkable blocks. The introduction came from inside the community. Chuck Makatura, a lifelong local and owner of Skeggy's Axe House downtown, lived the problem as a resident and as a business owner, and connected LongFi Solutions with the City of Easton.

Dead texts and dropped calls

When the crowd peaks, messages will not send and calls will not connect.

Families lose each other

Parents and kids cannot regroup when the square is shoulder to shoulder.

Merchant payments slow

Card readers and point of sale systems on cellular slow or time out when downtown is busiest.

Per event fixes do not scale

The festivals return year after year, so downtown needs infrastructure that is ready each time.

The Solution

LongFi Connect uses the Wi-Fi Easton already has at Centre Square to improve cell phone service.

LongFi enabled LongFi Connect on the city's existing Wi-Fi at The Circle in Centre Square. When someone on a participating carrier walks into the coverage area, the phone authenticates on its own using Passpoint, with no password, no splash page, and no app to download.

  • Runs on the Wi-Fi in place. A configuration on the network the city already owns at Centre Square.
  • Connects on the SIM. Phones attach on their own, with no app, no password, and no portal login.
  • A Passpoint configuration. LongFi Connect uses Passpoint, or Hotspot 2.0, and the carrier credentials already on the device.
  • Tuned for the crowd. Set for crowd density rather than everyday load, holding voice grade quality when the streets fill.
  • Secure by design. Guest traffic is authenticated, encrypted, and kept apart from city systems.

A full festival season, carried without the failures.
