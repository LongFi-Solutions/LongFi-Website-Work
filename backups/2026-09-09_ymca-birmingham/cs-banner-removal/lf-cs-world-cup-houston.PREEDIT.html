The Challenge

When a district fills up, phones lose the signal fans rely on.

On the biggest match days, families lose track of each other, a text will not send, and the tap-to-pay stalls right at the counter, the moments that matter most in a crowd.

East Downtown Houston drew fans by the thousands for the World Cup, from the fan festival out to the bars and rooftops nearby. LongFi Connect was there to carry the load and keep guests online through the rush.

Friends lose each other

In a shoulder-to-shoulder crowd, people cannot regroup or check in fast.

Payments and tickets stall

Tap-to-pay and mobile tickets time out at the worst possible moment.

Moments go unshared

Fans miss the chance to film and post the match and the day out.

The Solution

LongFi Connect improves indoor cell connectivity using the venue's excess Wi-Fi capacity.

Across East Downtown, LongFi Connect ran on the Wi-Fi the venues already had. When someone on a participating mobile carrier walked into the coverage area, the phone connected on its own, with no password, no splash page, and no app to download.

  • Runs on the Wi-Fi in place. A configuration on the networks the East Downtown venues already run.
  • Connects on the SIM. Phones attach on their own for compatible devices, with no app and no password.
  • OpenRoaming ready. Because the network already supported Passpoint, adding the Wireless Broadband Alliance identifiers took minutes.
  • Secure by design. Guest traffic is authenticated, encrypted, and kept separate from the venue's own systems.
