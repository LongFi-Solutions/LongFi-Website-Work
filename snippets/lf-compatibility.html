Pillar one

Room on your internet

The venue needs some spare capacity on its internet connection. LongFi Connect rides on a small share of what is already there, so day to day traffic keeps priority.

  • A business broadband connection that is not already maxed out during busy hours.
  • As a starting point, about 25 Mbps of spare download and 10 Mbps of spare upload.
  • The LongFi Connect lane typically uses only a small share of the available bandwidth.
  • Steady, low latency internet works best. A fast, stable line matters more than a very large one.
25 / 10 Mbps A practical starting point for spare download and upload. Final fit is confirmed during a short site check. See the technical best practices.

Pillar three

Access to your dashboard

LongFi needs admin access to the Wi‑Fi controller or dashboard so the carrier approved setup can be applied to the network. Nothing else on the network is changed.

  • Admin access to the controller or dashboard that runs the venue Wi‑Fi.
  • Different vendors name it differently. Some call it a controller, others a dashboard or a control center.
  • An I.T. contact or managed service provider can grant this access on the venue behalf.
  • The LongFi team applies the setup and keeps the existing systems on the network untouched.
