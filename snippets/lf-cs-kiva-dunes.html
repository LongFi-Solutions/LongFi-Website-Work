The Challenge

Cell coverage can vary on the Fort Morgan peninsula.

Kiva Lodge holds 23 condominium units, the golf shop and Kiva Grill, a public restaurant and bar. Overnight guests, golfers and diners pass through the same building, and the area welcomed 7.14 million visitors in 2025.1 When a phone stalls indoors, the small plans of a visit stall with it.

1 Gulf Shores & Orange Beach Tourism, 2025 Tourism Economic Impact, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach and Fort Morgan.

Arrival details

Guests pull up unit details, directions and check-in notes as they reach the lodge.

Plans with the group

Diners settle tee times, dinner plans and the next stop from the table.

Guest Wi-Fi takes steps

Joining means finding a network name and a password, and many guests do not think to switch.

The Solution

LongFi Connect improves indoor cell connectivity using the lodge's excess Wi-Fi capacity.

CBI already managed the property's Omada network: EAP720 Wi-Fi 7 ceiling-mount access points and an OC200 hardware controller. With LongFi's step-by-step guide, CBI activated LongFi Connect as a configuration change on that network, and no new hardware was required.

  • Automatic for guests. Compatible phones with eligible carrier credentials connect on their own, with no password and no sign-in page.
  • Built on Omada. LongFi Connect runs on the Omada access points and controller CBI already manages.
  • Secure by design. Guest traffic is authenticated, encrypted and kept separate from the resort's own systems.
  • CBI stays in control. CBI keeps managing the network, and LongFi monitors performance and reports the results.
