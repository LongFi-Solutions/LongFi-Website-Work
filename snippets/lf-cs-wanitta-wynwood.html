The Challenge

Tourists reached the register, and the connection dropped.

Wanitta draws shoppers from around the world, many traveling without a local data plan. Inside the store, cell signal faded right at the moment of the sale.

Staff ended up sharing the store Wi-Fi password just so a visitor could pay or message someone, an awkward pause right before checkout.

Signal fades at the counter

Indoor cell coverage drops right where the sale happens.

Sharing the Wi-Fi password

Staff read out the password so a visitor can pay or message.

The sale stalls

Tap-to-pay and mobile wallets time out at the worst moment.

The Solution

LongFi Connect improves indoor cell signal using the store's spare Wi-Fi capacity.

Wanitta already ran business-grade Wi-Fi for the shop floor. LongFi Connect uses part of that spare capacity, so a shopper on a participating mobile carrier connects on their own, with no password, no splash page, and no app to download.

  • Runs on the Wi-Fi in place. A configuration on the network the store already runs.
  • Connects on the SIM. Phones attach on their own for compatible devices, with no app and no password.
  • Works with participating mobile carriers. Visitors from out of town connect without setup or sign-up.
  • Secure by design. Guest traffic is authenticated, encrypted, and kept separate from the store's own systems.
