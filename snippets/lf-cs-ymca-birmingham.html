The Challenge

Buildings designed for activity, not for cell signal.

Carriers do not build indoor infrastructure for community nonprofits, and a distributed antenna system runs well into six figures for a building this size. That leaves members working around the gaps on their own.

Saturday in the gymnasium

Cell signal struggles to reach inside the gym. Parents spend Saturdays at youth sports events, unable to get a call or text through.

The basics stop working

Members can struggle to place calls, send texts, and use app-based workouts from inside the building.

Guest Wi-Fi falls short

Guest Wi-Fi helps, but it is not a complete solution, since it adds friction for members to find it and join it manually.

The Solution

LongFi Connect improves indoor cell connectivity using the Y's excess Wi-Fi capacity.

It runs as a configuration on the business-grade Wi-Fi already in the building, so member phones connect on their own and keep working when the floor is full.

  • Automatic for members. Phones connect on their own, with no app, no password, and no portal.
  • Calls and texts, not just data. Carrier traffic is carried indoors across the Wi-Fi already in the building, so voice and SMS work like they would outside.
  • Secure by design. Member traffic is authenticated and encrypted, and kept separate from the Y's own systems.
  • No cost to the Y. Activation takes minimal I.T. time and runs quietly alongside operations.
