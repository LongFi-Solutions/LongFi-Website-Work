Questions about Relay

Who can use Relay?

Venue owners, operators and partners with a site on LongFi Connect.

How current is the data?

Usage data refreshes daily.

Does the venue need new Wi‑Fi equipment?

No new hardware is required when the Wi‑Fi environment is compatible, and compatibility is reviewed during site qualification. Relay reads from the same network, with nothing to install on site.

What if the invite is lost or sign-in fails?

Email support@longfisolutions.com with the venue name, and the LongFi team will resend the invite or reset access.
